02 / Better flow

Business Automation

NZ$800 / STARTING PRICE

For a business that wants to make one defined, repeatable task easier to manage using compatible digital tools.

Included in the package
  • One discovery session to understand the task and desired result
  • A simple workflow map showing the trigger, steps and handovers
  • Configuration of one agreed automation
  • Connection of up to 2 compatible apps/services, where supported
