04 / The studio

Good ideas deserve
a clear place to land.

NorthLight Studios is an independent New Zealand digital studio focused on helping small businesses present themselves clearly online and make practical improvements to the way work flows.

OUR POINT OF VIEW

Digital should feel considered, not complicated.

We care about the details people notice: a clear message, a thoughtful layout, a useful customer journey and a website that feels like it belongs to the business behind it.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND NORTHLIGHT

Two founders. One shared direction.

NorthLight Studios is led by Chantell Awheto and Tk Ngatai. We work collaboratively with each business to understand the goal, agree on the scope and shape a suitable digital solution.

HOW WE APPROACH PROJECTS

Clear expectations, practical delivery.

  • Understand the business and its customers
  • Recommend only what suits the agreed goal
  • Set out deliverables and exclusions before work begins
  • Explain limitations and hand over what was built
OUR DEMONSTRATIONS

Concepts are labelled honestly.

Any demonstration or concept work is identified as such. We do not present concept studies as commissioned client deployments or claim results that have not been verified.
