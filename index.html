02 / A clearer process

Less chasing.
More flow.

A workflow is the sequence of steps that moves a task from start to finish. We can help identify where a simple, suitable automation may save time or reduce missed handovers.

Example: a customer enquiry arrives → the details are captured → an acknowledgement is sent → the right person is notified → a follow-up reminder is created. The workflow page explains what this means and what it can — and cannot — automate.
